# Switch Knob Fix Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The knob of every switch in the menu is visibly a knob in both positions, so turning a switch on shows the knob at the right.

**Architecture:** One theme edit in `brand::theme()`: the switch thumb's background becomes `Role::Foreground` in every state (`Prop::fixed`), replacing lyra's dark checked `primary-foreground`, which the brand palette makes nearly the page colour. Tests render the switch through the menu's own raster and damage tracker.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 1.96, egui 0.37, shadcn-egui 0.8 (lyra), the menu's `raster::Canvas` and `damage::Tracker`.

**Spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-switch-design.md](../specs/2026-11-20-switch-design.md).

- No animation: `animation_time` stays 0 in `app.rs` and `osd/draw.rs`.

## Task 1: tests that pin the knob (red)

**Files:** `src/thinfloe-menu/src/brand.rs` (tests module).

Helper `switch_frames(clicks) -> Vec<(Canvas, Vec<ClippedPrimitive>, Rect)>`:
an `egui::Context` with `brand::apply` and `animation_time = 0.0`, a
200 x 100 screen, frames at `time` 0.0, 0.1, then the click frame (pointer
move, press, release at the track centre) at 0.2 and a frame at 0.3. Each
frame: `run_ui` with `ui.add(Switch::new(&mut on))`, `Textures::apply_set`,
`tessellate`, a fresh `Canvas::new(w, h, theme().palette().background)` and
`canvas.draw(.., canvas.bounds())`, `apply_free`. Returns the canvas, the
primitives and the track rect.

`knob(canvas, track, on) -> (Color32, egui::Rect)`: the knob colour is the
canvas pixel 4 px inside the track's left end (off) or right end (on), at
the track's vertical centre; the knob rect is the bounding box of the pixels
of that colour inside the track.

- [ ] `the_switch_knob_moves_and_reads_on_the_page`: off frame (index 1) and
  on frame (index 3). Assert the knob colours are equal; the on knob's centre
  x exceeds the off knob's by at least `track.width() / 3.0`; the contrast
  ratio (WCAG relative luminance, a small test helper) of the knob colour with
  `theme().palette().background` is at least 4.5 in both frames; the
  switch is on after the click.
- [ ] `a_switch_click_damages_the_old_and_new_knob`: feed the primitives of
  frames 1 and 2 to one `damage::Tracker` (ppp 1); the damage of frame 2
  contains both the off and the on knob rects.
- [ ] Run `cargo test brand::` : the first fails (knob colours differ, on
  knob contrast about 1.1), the second passes.
- [ ] Commit `test(menu): the switch knob moves and reads on the page`.

## Task 2: the knob is the foreground colour and inset (green)

**Files:** `src/thinfloe-menu/src/brand.rs` (`theme()`, constants
`SWITCH_KNOB` = 14 and `SWITCH_KNOB_INSET` = 2).

- [ ] After the two `radius` lines: a comment (lyra's dark checked knob is
  `primary-foreground`, near the page colour here, so on a square switch the
  knob looked like background and the ice half like a knob that stayed) and:
  `style.switch_thumb.bg = Prop::fixed(Paint::Role(Role::Foreground, 1.0));`,
  `width` and `height` `Prop::fixed(SWITCH_KNOB)`, `shift_x`
  `Prop::fixed(Shift::Px(SWITCH_KNOB_INSET))` with
  `.on(State::CHECKED, Shift::Calc(1.0, SWITCH_KNOB_INSET))`, and
  `style.switch.border_width = Prop::fixed(0.0)`: the track's transparent
  border is one device pixel at every scale and made the two gaps differ at a
  fractional scale; without it 2 + 14 + 14 + 2 is the track's 32 points.
  Focus still shows as lyra's ring outside the track.
- [ ] The raster test runs at scales 1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75 and 2, with the
  switch 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6 and 0.8 points from the left: a 2 px ring of track
  colour on all sides, equal gaps at whole scales and at most one pixel apart
  at fractional ones (the raster's pixel-centre rule; only a thumb snapped
  to the pixel grid in shadcn-egui would avoid it).
- [ ] `cargo test` (all green), `cargo clippy -- -D warnings`, `cargo fmt --check`.
- [ ] Commit `fix(menu): the switch knob is light in both positions and inset`.
